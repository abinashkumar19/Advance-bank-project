How a rupee moves

From tap to settled, in three hops

Every transaction takes the same path through the system — nothing skipped, nothing hidden.

01

You initiate

A transfer, payment, or card swipe is authorized against your account balance and any linked fraud rules, in real time.

02

It's routed and checked

The event is published once and picked up independently by fraud detection, notifications, and audit logging — each does its job without slowing the others down.

event: TransactionCompleted
03

It settles

Balances update, a receipt is generated, and the entry lands permanently on your statement — searchable the moment it's posted.
